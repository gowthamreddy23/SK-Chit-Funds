How Chit Fund Works

Understand the simple and transparent process of saving and bidding in SK Chit Funds.

Step 1: Join a Chit Plan

Select a chit plan (₹5,000 or ₹10,000) and complete the registration process. Each plan runs for a fixed number of months.

Step 2: Monthly Contribution

All members contribute a fixed monthly amount. The total collection for that month is given to one eligible member.

Step 3: Intimation Before Lifting

Members must inform the office 2–3 months in advance if they wish to lift the chit. Priority is given based on the order of confirmation.

Step 4: Lifting the Chit

Once approved, the member receives the chit amount for that month. After lifting the chit, the monthly contribution changes:

• ₹5,000 plan → ₹6,000 per month
• ₹10,000 plan → ₹12,000 per month

Step 5: Continue Until Completion

All members continue paying until the chit cycle is completed. Each member receives the chit amount once in the cycle.

Example – ₹5,000 Chit Plan (21 Months)

Step 1: 20 members join the ₹5,000 plan.

Step 2: Every month, each member pays ₹5,000.

Step 3: A member informs 2–3 months in advance to lift the chit.

Step 4: That member receives amount as shown in chit plans for that month.

After Lifting: The member will pay ₹6,000 per month for the remaining months.

Step 5: The process continues until all 21 months are completed and every member receives the benefit once.

Before Lifting: ₹5,000 per month

After Lifting: ₹6,000 per month
